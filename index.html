Every Sale Counts. Every Sale Must Be Verified.

All qualifying DMC sales made through SmartEarn, Selar, and Nestuge during the official challenge period will be tracked using the registered email addresses of participating affiliates.

To ensure accurate tracking and fair reward allocation, affiliates must use their registered email addresses across the participating platforms.

Only eligible sales recorded within the official challenge period — October 19, 2026, to January 31, 2027 — will count toward an affiliate's total qualifying sales.

Affiliates are responsible for ensuring that their registered email addresses are accurate and consistent with the details required for challenge tracking.

Important tracking rule: Your registered email address is the key to tracking your sales across platforms. Make sure your details are correct so your qualifying sales can be properly attributed to you.

You Win a Category. You Get That Category's Prize.

Each affiliate will receive the prize specifically assigned to the highest eligible sales category they qualify for at the end of the challenge.

Prizes are not cumulative. Reaching a higher sales milestone does not entitle an affiliate to collect the prizes assigned to lower milestones.

For example:

  • An affiliate who makes 50 qualifying sales receives the prize assigned to the 50-sales category.
  • An affiliate who makes 600 qualifying sales receives the 600-sales prize package only — an all-expenses-paid trip to Seychelles plus the applicable cash prize.
  • The 600-sales winner does not receive the separate 500-sales prize in addition to the 600-sales package.
  • An affiliate who makes 1,500 qualifying sales receives the complete prize package assigned to the 1,500-sales category, not the prizes from the 1,000-sales or lower categories.

The same principle applies to every milestone in the challenge.

Your final qualifying sales total determines your reward category. You receive the prize assigned to that category only.
